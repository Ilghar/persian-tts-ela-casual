# Pitch-Matched Clip Verification (Stage 8)

Verification of short clips from listed sources against Liora (bright high-girly for Ela) and Ara (softer breathier for Pegah).

## Source Overview

### OpenNSFW SFX / Voice Packs
- Large collaborative pack with multiple female moan packs (GeminiStarSign1, Rain_va_, VelvetSheetzVA, Hoshinomeririri, etc.).
- Contains wet sounds, plaps, oral, body noises, and dedicated moan packs.
- License: Creative Commons Attribution (credit required).
- Recommendation: Prefer higher-pitched, shorter, brighter clips for Liora; softer, more drawn-out clips for Ara. Avoid deep or raspy packs for either character.

### MagicalMysticVA Free Samples (itch.io / Freesound)
- High Pitched Female Moan Sound Effect — good candidate for Liora (bright, higher).
- Soft / Breathy / Cute Lewd Breaths — good candidates for Ara.
- Cute Raspy / Mommy Voice — generally avoid for both (too raspy or mature).
- License: Free for personal/commercial with credit appreciated.

### Pixabay Soft Jazz
- Multiple soft instrumental jazz tracks (Silky Nights, Moonlight Pickings, etc.).
- No vocals — suitable as low-volume background for both characters.
- License: Pixabay Content License (free for use).

### Bed Creak / Wet Sounds
- Freesound and SoundDino have short bed creak clips (1–40s).
- OpenNSFW has dedicated wet and in/out sounds.
- These are non-vocal and can be used for both characters; tempo should be matched to scene.

## Practical Selection Rules

1. For Ela/Liora: select clips described as high-pitched, bright, short, or cute (avoid raspy or mature).
2. For Pegah/Ara: select clips described as soft, breathy, drawn-out, or calm (avoid sharp high peaks).
3. Always test by ear against a short Liora or Ara TTS sample before full mix.
4. Non-vocal body and background clips are shared but must match tempo and stay under dialogue.
5. Credit all OpenNSFW and MagicalMysticVA clips when used.

## Limitations of This Verification

Actual audio files were not downloaded or spectrogram-analyzed in this environment. Verification is based on available descriptions, pack categories, and known voice characteristics of Liora (bright high-girly) and Ara (softer breathier). Final pitch match must be confirmed by listening when clips are obtained.
