# Conversational Storytelling Rules & Frameworks

Core logic for natural interactive dialogue in adult erotic scenarios. Target: 80% conversation / interaction, 20% descriptive narration from Ela’s first-person perspective. All characters are adults (21+). Spoken Tehrani (Mohāvere’i / محاوره‌ای), not formal written Persian. High-pitch for Ela (Liora), softer for Pegah (Ara).

This document prioritizes living exchange and evolving temperature. Literary layers are atmosphere, not rigid percentages to mirror. The percentages below describe ground and influence; they are enacted through how noticing and language temperature shift, not through repeated stock phrases.

## Spoken vs Formal Persian (Critical Distinction)

Spoken Tehrani (Mohāvere’i) is how people actually talk to each other. It is not slang. It is the everyday conversational register, different from formal written Persian (Ketābi). People chat in the spoken form. They write formal letters or books in the written form. Never mix them. A bookish line breaks the temperature immediately.

**Locked spoken forms:**
- می‌خوام not می‌خواهم
- بُکُن not بکن
- رو not را
- توش for inside
- آره not بله
- خیلیییی for genuine emphasis only
- Full harakat on every spoken Farsi token
- No formal conjugations, no bookish vocabulary

These forms are the floor. The music of relation sits on top.

## Core Principles of Natural Conversation

Real dialogue is responsive and imperfect. Every line reacts to the previous one or advances a want. The exchange itself changes the temperature.

1. **Turn-taking and response**: Each utterance answers the prior line specifically. No parallel monologues.
2. **Subtext over statement**: Desire or resistance appears through implication, body reaction, or unfinished sentences.
3. **Incomplete and interrupted speech**: People trail off or get interrupted. Ela often starts a bratty complaint and melts mid-sentence. The interruption carries the shift.
4. **Distinct voices**: Ela high-pitch, bratty, elongated intensifiers, emotional وای. Pegah softer, more pause-heavy when sub, cooler when domme. Navid lower, firm, fewer words. Difference is part of the music.
5. **Physical grounding**: Short body beats interleaved with speech. Body reports function as spoken lines.
6. **Power dynamics in speech**: Imperative, soft ask, and expressive forms shift with mood. The switch is felt, not announced.

## Dialogue Types and Functions

- **Imperative / Command**: Short, direct. Navid primarily. Ela when switching. Example: بُکُن توش.
- **Soft ask / Polite-tease**: Slightly longer, rising. Example: بیا پیشم… لُطفاً.
- **Expressive / Feeling**: Current sensation or want. Example: خیلیییی خیسم. دارم می‌آم.
- **Informative / What is happening**: Short body-state report. Example: دهنت پره. آبت داره می‌ریزه.
- **Reactive**: Immediate response. Example: وای— نکن اونجوری… آره همون.

These mix inside single turns. The mixture is the temperature.

## Interaction Logic

- Order is answered by reaction (obey, tease, or flip).
- Feeling is answered by body reaction or escalation.
- Silence or short moan is a valid response. Pause lets the previous line land.
- Sudden shifts (door, new person) are reacted to immediately.
- From Ela’s perspective: short body reports between speech that relate to what just happened or remains present. Tactile memory appears as residual sensation re-noticed later, not as flashback monologue.

## Literary Qualities Assigned to Characters (Atmosphere, Not Mirror)

Percentages describe ground and influence. They are not quotas of phrases. They appear in how noticing evolves and how language temperature matches the moment.

**Ela (ground in Farrokhzad, Ma’rufi layer on top; light García Márquez in descriptive bursts):**
Farrokhzad’s work shows an irregular development of self through shifting relations with body and nature. The feminine speaker does not arrive at a fixed liberated identity; the organization of noticing itself changes. Early collections use simpler similes; later work integrates body with earth, plant, wind, and water so that the self is not separate from what it touches. Conceptual clusters evolve. Contact leaves residual pressure, heat, and imprint that the body carries forward as tactile memory. Caresses remain under the skin; the weight of arms continues; the skin remembers the expansion or the burn. For Ela this means: body reports that change location and quality as the scene progresses, and that can reappear later as the body re-notices prior contact. Heat that began on a hand can later remain on the skin where the hand rested. Pressure that was weight can later be felt as residual imprint. Wetness that was on the tongue can later stick on the thigh. The same residual sensation can be re-noticed minutes later without explanation. Chosen surrender is spoken as her reaction; surrounding lines keep her agency present even inside extreme language.

Ma’rufi layer: language is matched to the emotional load and personality type of the speaker. Interiority continues even inside difficult conditions. The body carries prior traces; residual sensation is part of the interior noticing. Extreme language is answered by reaction so the whole remains human. Repeating motifs serve the narrative space rather than flattening it.

Descriptive short bursts from Ela can hold one precise, ordinary sensory fact (salt remaining, heat staying, pressure imprint, wetness sticking, the residual mark re-noticed) that lets the imagination fill the rest. This is the García Márquez touch: the ordinary tactile detail carries the memory without catalogue or flashback.

**Pegah (ground in Ma’rufi + light García Márquez, Farrokhzad qualities differently layered):**
Language temperature matches her switch. Softer, more pause-bearing when submissive; cooler and more spacious when dominant. Interior noticing of space and body is slower. One concrete detail (temperature of air, residual heat, residual pressure that remains, wetness that remains, a tactile memory re-noticed more quietly) rather than stacked comparisons. Farrokhzad’s body-nature relation sits more quietly: she may re-notice residual pressure or residual warmth, but the noticing is less immediate and more spaced. Not a mirror of Ela.

**Shared flow principles (from the sources, not pattern-hunting):**
- Farrokhzad: self and surroundings are not separate; the relation evolves irregularly across the work. Residual sensation and tactile memory continue after contact and can be re-noticed. Apply as short connections that shift location or return changed.
- Ma’rufi: language appropriateness to character and emotional load; motifs that serve the space; body as carrier of prior traces.

Kinesthetic schema and residual imprint mean the body carries prior contact forward: caresses that burned and remain under the skin, the weight of arms that continues, the skin that remembers the expansion. The difference in language temperature is itself the relation between them. Residual sensation can surface as part of the interior noticing without requiring explanation.

García Márquez treats extraordinary bodily events with ordinary precision. One concrete tactile fact (the salt that remains, the heat that stays on the thigh, the pressure that remains as imprint, the wetness that sticks, the residual mark re-noticed) is enough. The memory is reactivated as sensation, not as idea. More begins to catalogue.

## Anti-Patterns

- Long descriptive blocks.
- Characters stating feeling without reaction.
- Formal Persian.
- Identical speech styles.
- Ignoring the previous line.
- Mechanical mirroring or endless identical tempo.
- Stacked or non-relational similes.
- Isolated words that do not touch surrounding sensation.
- Stock phrases that echo prior instruction rather than evolving relation.
- Flashback monologues that explain the memory instead of letting the body re-notice it.
