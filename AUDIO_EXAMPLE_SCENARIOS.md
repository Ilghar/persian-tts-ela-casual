## Intensity Coding Notes

- Slow: longer pauses, softer wet sounds, longer breaths.
- Medium: regular rhythm.
- Fast/hard: shorter sharper body sounds, strained voice.
- Near peak: rising or drawn-out moans, broken speech.
- Spatial always indicated in the tag.
