## Usage Notes

- Prefer CC-BY or royalty-free sources.
- For scenario generation: reference by category + character + intensity rather than hard-coding specific files until clips are selected.
- Crop or layer in post if needed; TTS handles speech, external audio handles pure moans/body/bg.
