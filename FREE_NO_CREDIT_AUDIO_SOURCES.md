# Free No-Attribution Audio Sources (Alternative to OpenNSFW)

OpenNSFW packs use Creative Commons Attribution and require credit. The following alternatives do not require attribution.

## Recommended No-Credit Sources

### SoundDino
- Free woman moaning sounds: https://sounddino.com/en/effects/woman-moaning/
- Explicitly listed as Royalty-Free, No Attribution required.
- Short clips available (7s–45s range).
- Suitable for selecting higher-pitched clips for Liora and softer ones for Ara by ear.

### Freesoundsite Collections
- Multiple collections labeled “completely free to download, with no copyright restrictions and no attribution required.”
- Examples: Female Moan, Woman Moaning, Orgasmic Female Moan collections.
- Browse and select short clips matching brightness for Liora or softness for Ara.

### Freesound CC0 Clips
- Search Freesound for “female moan” filtered by Creative Commons 0 (CC0).
- Examples include anonymous CC0 recordings of female moans and bed sounds.
- No attribution required.

### Pixabay
- Soft instrumental jazz tracks remain free under Pixabay Content License with no attribution required for most uses.

## Usage Notes

- Prefer SoundDino and Freesoundsite collections first for no-credit moans.
- Always verify the specific license on the download page before use.
- Select by ear against a short Liora or Ara TTS sample for pitch match.
- Non-vocal body sounds (bed creak, wet) from the same no-attribution sources can be used freely.
- OpenNSFW remains available if credit is acceptable, but is no longer required.

## Updated Recommendation

For the multi-layer pipeline, prioritize the no-attribution sources above. Credit is not required when using SoundDino, qualifying Freesoundsite collections, or Freesound CC0 clips.

This replaces the previous credit requirement for OpenNSFW in the workflow.
